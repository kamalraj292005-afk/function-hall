Welcome to Palani Murugan Hall

The Perfect Auspicious Venue For Your Grand Celebrations

உங்கள் குடும்பத்தின் சுப நிகழ்வுகளுக்கு சிறந்த இடம்

Palani Murugan Function Hall is an elegantly appointed wedding and celebration venue located on the main Aranthangi to Peravurani Road at Vallavari, situated conveniently near the sacred Ayanar Kovil Temple.

Designed with spacious traditional architecture and modern conveniences, our hall provides an enchanting environment for weddings, receptions, betrothals (engagements), ear-piercing ceremonies, birthday celebrations, and family gatherings.

  • Centrally located on the main highway road with smooth vehicle access
  • Spacious marriage hall with dedicated dining and modern kitchen facilities
  • Dedicated generator backup ensuring uninterrupted illumination and sound
  • Ample designated parking area for buses, cars, and two-wheelers
  • Transparent pricing with just ₹5,000 booking advance
